# SEO Strategy for Invoice Port

## Target Keywords (High Priority)

1. **Free invoicing software** - High volume, high intent
2. **Invoicing software for freelancers** - Specific audience
3. **Invoicing software for small business** - Specific audience
4. **Free invoice generator** - High volume
5. **GST invoice software** - India-specific, high intent
6. **Online invoicing software** - Broad, high volume
7. **Invoice maker** - High volume
8. **Billing software for freelancers** - Specific audience
9. **Professional invoice templates** - Long-tail
10. **Invoice management software** - Specific audience

## Competitor Analysis

- **Wave**: Free, strong brand, but cluttered UX
- **Zoho Invoice**: Feature-rich, but complex for beginners
- **Invoice Ninja**: Open-source, technical audience
- **FreshBooks**: Premium positioning, $19+/month
- **Agiled**: Bundled solution, starting free

## Our Competitive Advantages

1. **Free forever** - No credit card required
2. **GST-compliant** - India-focused
3. **Simple UX** - Faster than competitors
4. **10 professional templates** - More than most free options
5. **Email integration** - Built-in, not add-on
6. **No processing fees** - Unlike Wave/PayPal

5. Improve internal linking structure
